Q&A primer

Q: How is this not Yelp + Hootsuite?
Both products are 15 years old, neither is multimodal, neither generates content, neither plans whole evenings. Eve is the AI-native rebuild of both at once with the marketplace flywheel between them.
Q: How do you bootstrap the diner side without restaurant supply?
Day-one diner queries don't require any restaurant supply — Eve generates plans grounded in Gemini's knowledge of the Bay Area. The Eve Original badge is the upgrade that kicks in once supply is there. Restaurants get a free trial — Eve-quality content packs are the carrot.
Q: What about Toast?
Toast is a POS company that bolted on marketing as an upsell. Their content tools are template-based and nobody uses them. We're a marketing-first product that integrates with Toast/Square/Resy as data sources, not a competitor on POS.
Q: How do you handle hallucinated restaurants?
Skeleton generation is grounded with Gemini's Google Search tool. We surface published restaurants (Eve Originals) with priority. v1.1 wires in Google Places API for ground-truth verification.
Q: Why solo? Will you raise?
Yes. 12 AI apps shipped as side projects this year alone. 120+ patents. I know how to ship and scale. Series Seed to bring on a brand designer, an SF restaurant partnerships lead, and an iOS engineer.
Q: Defensibility?
Vertical prompt corpus tuned by a vegetarian foodie founder; restaurant brand memory (cuisine, voice, signature dishes) creates lock-in; the marketplace flywheel makes the index grow with every published special. None of these compound for a horizontal AI tool.

Timing notes

  • Pre-step takes ~45 sec. Do it during the previous team's pitch.
  • 5 minutes is tight. Practice the transitions (form-fill → button click → wait → narrate while loading).
  • The voice playback at 1:30 is the most powerful moment. Make sure speakers are loud enough.
  • If something fails live: keep narrating the tab next to it. Never apologize. The pitch deck is the architecture/business fallback.

Backup if live demo fails

Open the pitch deck. Walk through slides 1 → 2 → 3. Then slides 4 → 5. Total time still under 5 min.
