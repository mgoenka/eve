Q&A primer

Q: How is this not Yelp + Hootsuite?

Both are 15 years old, neither is multimodal, neither generates content, neither plans whole evenings. Eve is the AI-native rebuild of both with the marketplace flywheel between them.

Q: How do you bootstrap diners without restaurant supply?

Day-one queries don't need supply — Eve plans grounded in Gemini's Bay Area knowledge. Eve Original badge is the upgrade once supply is there. Restaurants get a free trial; the content packs are the carrot.

Q: What about Toast?

Toast is POS that bolted on marketing. Templates, nobody uses them. Eve is marketing-first. Integrates with Toast/Square/Resy as data sources, doesn't compete on POS.

Q: How do you handle hallucinated restaurants?

Skeleton generation grounded with Gemini's Google Search tool. Eve Originals (verified, restaurant-published) get badge priority. v1.1 wires in Google Places API for ground-truth.

Q: Why solo? Will you raise?

Yes. 12 AI apps shipped this year. 120+ patents. I know how to ship and scale. Series Seed for a brand designer, an SF restaurant partnerships lead, an iOS engineer.

Q: Defensibility?

Vertical prompt corpus tuned by a vegetarian foodie founder; restaurant brand memory creates lock-in; the marketplace flywheel grows the index with every special. Horizontal AI tools don't compound any of these.

If something fails live

  • Mic doesn't catch: use the text box. Same plan, just slower.
  • Image doesn't render: keep narrating the next stop. Eve will catch up.
  • Voice doesn't play: point at the transcript bubble. The text is on screen.
  • Whole demo fails: open architecture page. Walk through SVG flow + endpoints. Same 5 minutes.

Never apologize. Keep talking. The story works without any one moving piece.
